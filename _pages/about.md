I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Materials Intelligence Lab at [LG AI Research](https://www.lgresearch.ai/), where I work on a materials foundation model that integrates organic and inorganic materials.
I received my Ph.D. from KAIST, where I was advised by Prof. [Chanyoung Park](https://dsail.kaist.ac.kr/) in the Department of Industrial & Systems Engineering.

My research is on graph neural networks, with a focus on explainability and multimodal learning, and on AI for science and engineering, including physics AI, mesh-based simulation, and circuit graphs.
From November 2025 to April 2026, I was a visiting researcher in the Graph and Geometric Learning Lab at Yale University, hosted by Prof. Rex Ying.
